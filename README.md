# FPS Storytelling Game

An original first-person shooter game concept focused on cinematic storytelling, immersive environments, tactical combat, and mission-driven gameplay.

## 🎮 Project Overview

This project explores the development of a narrative-driven FPS game inspired by the cinematic presentation and mission-based structure of modern military shooter games.

The goal is to create an engaging single-player experience combining intense combat, memorable characters, strategic missions, and a compelling storyline.

## 🎯 Core Vision

- Cinematic and immersive storytelling.
- Realistic and engaging FPS mechanics.
- Mission-based progression.
- Detailed environments and world-building.
- Interesting characters, alliances, and conflicts.
- A balance between action, exploration, and narrative.

## 📖 Story & Narrative

The game's central storyline, setting, conflicts, and character journeys are currently in the conceptual development stage.

Further details will be documented as the narrative evolves.

## 🌍 World & Setting

The game world will feature carefully designed environments, mission locations, military installations, urban areas, and other locations relevant to the storyline.

## 👥 Characters & Factions

Character profiles, backgrounds, motivations, alliances, and opposing factions will be developed as part of the world-building process.

## 🎯 Missions & Objectives

The game is planned around structured missions, each contributing to the larger narrative and introducing different gameplay challenges.

## ⚙️ Gameplay Mechanics

Planned areas of exploration:

- First-person movement and controls.
- Weapon handling and combat.
- Enemy AI and tactical behaviour.
- Mission objectives and progression.
- Environmental interaction.
- Cinematic sequences.

## 🔫 Weapons & Equipment

Weapon categories, equipment, attachments, and gameplay balancing will be explored during development.
